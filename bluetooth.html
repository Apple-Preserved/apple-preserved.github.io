Bluetooth Permissions

This lets Apple Preserves remember your board.

  1. Click the address bar at the top of this window, press Ctrl+V, then Enter.
    If nothing appears, click Copy and try again: chrome://flags/#enable-web-bluetooth-new-permissions-backend
  2. Set Use the new permissions backend for Web Bluetooth to Enabled.
  3. Click Restart at the bottom. Chrome restarts and Apple Preserves comes back. Pick your board once more; after that it's remembered.
